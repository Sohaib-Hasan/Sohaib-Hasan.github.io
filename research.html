Theses

Graduate research

MPHIL · CUST ISLAMABAD · 2018–2021

Generalized Signcryption Based on Elliptic Curve Cryptography

Extends an efficient ECC-based signcryption scheme into a generalized construction that operates in signcryption, signature-only, or encryption-only mode as required, with full analysis against chosen-plaintext, chosen-ciphertext, forgery, and man-in-the-middle attacks.

MSC · COMSATS ATTOCK · 2015–2017

Flow of Eyring-Powell Fluid in a Channel Using Adomian Decomposition Method

Derives a series solution for steady, isothermal channel flow of a non-Newtonian Eyring-Powell fluid, with velocity profile, flow rate, and stream function obtained via the Adomian Decomposition Method.
