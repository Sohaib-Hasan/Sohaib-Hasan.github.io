About

The full story.

Nine years in the classroom, two theses in very different corners of mathematics, and a growing interest in how AI systems reason.

I'm a Mathematics Lecturer and Head of the Mathematics Department at Horizon Degree & Commerce College in Chakwal, Pakistan — the same college where I earned my own BSc in Mathematics. I've spent over nine years teaching undergraduate students, across subjects from Real Analysis and Abstract Algebra to Numerical Analysis and Machine Learning, and in that time I've taught more than 1,500 students.

My academic path has taken me through two very different research areas: fluid dynamics for my MSc, and elliptic-curve cryptography for my MPhil, which I completed while already teaching full-time. More recently, that same interest in rigorous problem-solving has pulled me toward AI mathematical reasoning — I competed in Kaggle's AI Mathematical Olympiad (AIMO3), placing in the top 12% of teams globally.

Outside the classroom, I run PlotLab, where I build Manim animations that turn abstract mathematics into something visual — and I'm currently applying to PhD programs in Data Science, Applied Mathematics, and Statistics.
