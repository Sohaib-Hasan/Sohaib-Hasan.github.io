I teach undergraduate mathematics at Horizon Degree & Commerce College in Chakwal, Pakistan, and I'm a section leader for Stanford's Probability for AI, where I teach top tech minds, researchers, and working professionals from around the world.

My own path went through an MSc in fluid dynamics and an MPhil in Mathematics researching elliptic-curve cryptography, and more recently toward AI systems that can reason mathematically.

I'm currently applying to PhD programs in Data Science, Applied Mathematics, and Statistics. Alongside teaching and research, I run PlotLab, where I build mathematical animations followed by 100K+ people on Facebook and Instagram, plus a YouTube channel, PlotLab Algorithms.
